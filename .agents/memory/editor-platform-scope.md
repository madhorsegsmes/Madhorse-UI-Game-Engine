The Madhorse editor is for PC use, not phone use, even though it can make mobile games.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the addition of phone-style Play Test controls.

**How to apply:** Prioritize keyboard, mouse, and PC workspace layouts. Do not add phone editor controls or touch-gameplay overlays to the editor. Preserve mobile-game authoring and output capabilities; editor device support and game target platforms are separate concerns.
